`update_return`) that currently hard-code `let user_id_opt: Option<i64> = None;`. This is the

recording or D-03 undo logic is added here; that is exclusively Plan 40-09's job.

"кем" column is never silently "система" for a real acting Manager/Admin.

`tauri_cmds/acts.rs`'s four corresponding `build_acts_*` functions pass it through.

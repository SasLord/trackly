<objective>
Build the `place_movements` repository — the single shared write-side helper
(`record_movement_if_applicable`) that every one of the seven write sites (device manual, cartridge
manual, cartridge transition main + nested auto-return, act create/update/do_return/update_return, and
D-28's bulk move) will call, plus the read-side query (`get_history`) that both the timeline (Plan
40-10) and printer detail (Plan 40-17) consume. Centralizing the D-04/D-06 guard and the actor/path
snapshot logic HERE means every downstream write-site plan is a one-line call, not a re-implementation
— directly preventing the "5 copies, 1 forgot to degrade softly" shape that caused IN-01 in Phase 39.2.

Purpose: give every later write-site plan (40-07/08/09/13) and every read-side plan (40-10/40-11) a
tested, reusable data-access layer instead of each reinventing insert/guard/snapshot logic.
Output: `place_movements_sqlite.rs` with insert/guard/delete/read functions; ordering unit test.
</objective>

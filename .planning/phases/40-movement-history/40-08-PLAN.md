<objective>
Wire BOTH cartridge write sites (manual `update` and `transition`/`transition_in_tx`, including its
easy-to-miss nested auto-return branch) to Plan 40-05's `record_movement_if_applicable` helper, using
the caller threading and before-fetch built in Plan 40-04. This plan is where Pitfall 3 (RESEARCH.md's
"second, easy-to-miss write site") gets its dedicated test.

movement row when the operation ACTUALLY changes `place_id` (e.g. installing into a printer at a
different place), never as a side effect of the status transition itself.
Output: `cartridge_service::update` and `cartridges_sqlite::transition_in_tx` (both call sites) record
movements; a dedicated test proving the nested auto-return produces its own separate row.
</objective>

<objective>
Wire all four act mutation methods (create/update/do_return/update_return, caller-threaded in Plan
40-06) to record movements with `act_id` set, and wire D-03's undo-scoped deletion into
`delete_soft`'s existing per-act LIFO cascade loop. This plan carries the two highest-risk pitfalls in
RESEARCH.md that are specific to acts: Pitfall 4 (place→NULL on return is a REAL reachable code path,
not hypothetical) and Pitfall 5 (the DELETE must sit inside each loop iteration, not as one blanket
delete at the end).

Purpose: make HST-03 real — every act-driven place change is linked to its act number, and deleting an
act correctly un-happens its movement history without leaving orphaned or wrongly-scoped rows.
Output: create/update/do_return/update_return all record movements correctly (including the D-06
NULL-skip); delete_soft's cascade loop deletes each act's own movement rows at the correct point.
</objective>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| act_service mutation methods → place_movements | Internal — `source`/`act_id` always server-derived from which method fired, never client-supplied |
| act_service::delete_soft → place_movements DELETE | Internal, scoped strictly by `act_id`, no user-controlled WHERE clause beyond the already-authorized act id |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-40-19 | Repudiation | A deleted handover's movement history disappearing without an audit trail of the deletion itself | accept | This is the explicit, user-approved D-03 behavior ("никакой компенсирующей записи не создаётся") — the act's own soft-delete already leaves an `audit_log` row documenting the deletion event; `place_movements` is deliberately NOT a durable audit trail for undone acts |
| T-40-20 | Denial of Service / Data Integrity | Pitfall 5's ordering bug (wrong-scoped delete) silently corrupting another act's history | mitigate | Task 4's `place_movements_act_undo_deletes` test explicitly seeds a control act and asserts its rows survive, directly catching cross-act scoping errors |
</threat_model>

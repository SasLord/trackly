<objective>
Close the confirmed BLOCKER gap from 24-VERIFICATION.md: `Badge.svelte`'s `appearance="count"` styling exists
only for the neutral default (`.badge-m-count`) and accent (`.badge-m-accent.badge-m-count`) tones. The
`success`/`warning`/`danger` tone blocks have no matching `&.badge-m-count` rule, so
`<Badge variant="success|warning|destructive" appearance="count">` falls through to the bare `.badge-m-count`
selector and renders neutral grey regardless of the requested tone — reproducible directly in the showcase's
own `BadgeSection.svelte` (lines 34/44/54) and confirmed absent in the compiled CSS.

Purpose: CMP-03 requires all 5 tones to render correctly across all 4 appearance variants (20 cells total).
Shipping only 2/5 tones for `count` silently breaks any future count-badge usage (unread counts, cartridge
totals, etc.) that needs a non-neutral/non-accent tone.
Output: `Badge.svelte` with tone-correct `count` CSS for all 5 tones.
</objective>

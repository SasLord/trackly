<objective>
Migrate the Cartridges entity's five transition operations (Install/ReturnToStock/ToRefill/
FromRefill/WriteOff) off freeform `location: String` onto `place_id: Option<i64>`, removing
`upsert_location_in_tx` (the cartridge-side twin of `resolve_location_id_in_tx`) and wiring
Install's printer-place default.

Purpose: cartridges get the same "caller passes a validated place_id, no implicit create" contract
Plan 06 established for devices — D-12/D-13's core mechanics. This plan also extends `search` with
a live place-path join (D-29/PLC-05, the cartridge-side sibling of Plan 06's device fix — required
because `search`'s existing `like_hits` CTE still references the about-to-be-dropped
`c.location` column) and exposes `storage_place_ids()` over both transports (B4: it was previously
service-internal only).
Output: `cartridge_service.rs` and `cartridges_sqlite.rs` migrated onto `place_id`; `search` matches
cartridges by intrinsic fields OR live place-path substring; `cartridge_storage_place_ids` reachable
via Tauri and `/api/v1/cartridge_storage_place_ids`.
</objective>

Migrate the Devices entity off `locations`/`location_id` onto `place_id`, removing the implicit
get-or-create helper (`resolve_location_id_in_tx`) that RESEARCH.md Common Pitfall 4 flags as the
single biggest silent-trap in this phase's blast radius, and removing the whole
`AutocompleteField::Location` mechanism (superseded entirely by `PlacePicker`).

Purpose: devices become the reference implementation every other entity migration (acts,
cartridges) in this wave follows for "caller passes a validated place_id, no auto-create."
Output: `device_service.rs`, `devices_sqlite.rs`, `domain/devices.rs` migrated; `locations_autocomplete`
removed from both the service and (in Plan 12) the transport layer.
</objective>

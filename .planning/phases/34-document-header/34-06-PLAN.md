    provide (it cannot see overflow, overlap, or actual font rendering).

    2. In Settings → Организация, fill in: a multi-line "Полное юридическое наименование" (3
       lines, fictional — e.g. "Общество с ограниченной\nответственностью\n«Тестовая

       (3 lines as typed, not re-wrapped by the browser) → short name in parentheses → requisites
       in the exact new order, all centered in an ~80mm-wide column, Times New Roman throughout.

<objective>
Migrate `ActFormItemsTable.svelte`'s per-row device picker — currently a hand-rolled `<input>` + portaled
`<ul>` with ~500 lines of drill-in/keyboard/ARIA logic (Plans 18-04/18-05's work) — onto the new `Dropdown`

`dropdownAnchor` mechanics, does not regress AUTO-02/AUTO-05/D-12's keyboard-ARIA floor inside a real modal
(SC #5) — the phase's highest-risk regression surface, deliberately chosen over an easier pilot location
(D-05's own reasoning).
Output: `ActFormItemsTable.svelte` with its device-picker cell rewired onto `Dropdown`; ~250-350 lines of
now-redundant per-row drill-in/open/keyboard state and the old `:global(.dropdown--items ...)` CSS block
removed (superseded by `Dropdown`'s own internal state and styling).
